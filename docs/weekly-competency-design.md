# Weekly competency reporting

Product and user: students document an active OJT week; assigned coordinators read evidence; Super Admin monitors aggregate participation and perceived competency.

Surface: Operate. Reuse Inter, RTU navy/blue/gold tokens, shared dialogs and form controls. Use a spacious single-column report, focused question cards and compact analytic tables. Blue marks actions; gold marks keyboard focus. Avoid dashboard filler and decorative metrics.

Primary actions: Continue to competency check, Submit weekly report, Publish question set. Save draft is secondary. Failed requests retain all input; final submission has a persistent receipt.

Required states: loading, unconfigured questionnaire, no placement, no reports, filtered empty, saved draft, submitted, overdue, no activity, excused break, validation/network failure, stale edit conflict, upload failure, small-sample suppression and permission denial. Questionnaire progress is textual and visual, with Back and Save draft and close. Keyboard focus stays within the dialog. Mobile controls have 44px targets; reduced-motion is respected.

Report data: work performed, competencies applied, learning gained, challenges/support, next-week plan, optional private image evidence, common and course questions. Student answers are self-ratings, not verified performance or a graded quiz. Industry Partner school evaluations retain their own workflow and native scale.

Scope: app development only. No Obsidian/paper edits. Preserve current working-tree changes. Institutional publication of actual questions remains an explicit staff action; do not seed invented school instruments.
